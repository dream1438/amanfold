# Amanfold brand

The source for Amanfold's visual identity. Everything here is generated by the scripts in `tools/`, so the logo, app icons, UI icons and design tokens stay in sync.

Open `guide/index.html` in a browser for the full brand guide (logo use, colour, type, voice and tone, icons, interface basics and sample screens).

## The symbol: Keyhole a

A lowercase **a**, the first letter of the name, whose opening is a **keyhole** and whose top corner is **folded over**. *Aman* (أمان) is Arabic for safety: the keyhole says that what you keep here is locked and only you hold the key. The folded corner is the *fold*.

It ships as three drawings: full colour (Aman Green with a Mint fold, 32 px and above), one colour (the fold becomes a cut line; for black, white and print) and small (plain cut corner and a larger keyhole; 24 px and below).

## Quick rules

- **Colour:** Aman Green `#0B6E4F` is the brand colour, Mint `#9BE4C4` is the fold, Ink `#0C1F1A` is text and Paper `#F4F6F3` is the background. Amber `#F5B544` appears at most once per screen and never as text on light backgrounds (use Amber 700 `#8F590D`). Danger red `#B3261E` is for weak or leaked passwords and destructive actions only. On dark backgrounds the logo is white and buttons use Green 400 `#2CB183`.
- **Type:** Readex Pro (display, Latin and Arabic), IBM Plex Sans + IBM Plex Sans Arabic (interface), IBM Plex Mono (passwords and codes). All SIL Open Font License, all on Google Fonts.
- **Logo:** always use the logo files; the name is custom-drawn (one-storey letters a, hand-adjusted spacing) and cannot be typed in a font. Clear space equals the symbol's stem width, a quarter of its height. Symbol minimum 16 px (small drawing), logo minimum 96 px wide. Don't mirror (not even in Arabic), recolour, stretch, or add padlocks and shields.
- **Voice:** calm, plain, honest about limits, equal in every language. Tagline: *Everything you keep, safely folded.* / *كل ما تحفظه، مطويٌّ بأمان.*

## Rebuilding

```sh
pip install fonttools uharfbuzz pillow
python brand/tools/build.py      # logos, app icons, tokens (downloads Readex Pro on first run)
python brand/tools/icons.py      # UI icons
node brand/tools/render-png.js   # PNG exports (needs Playwright)
python brand/tools/favicon-ico.py
python brand/tools/guide.py      # brand guide page
```

Change colours in `tools/palette.py` and the symbol's geometry in `tools/mark.py`, then rebuild.
