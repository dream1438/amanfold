01

The idea

Amanfold joins two words. Aman (أمان) is Arabic for safety, trust and peace of mind. Fold is what you do to keep something private.

The symbol is the first letter of the name, a lowercase a, and it carries both words. The opening of the letter is a keyhole: that is aman, the promise that what you keep here is locked and only you hold the key. The top corner of the letter is folded over like a private note: that is fold.

It can be described in one sentence, "an a with a keyhole and a folded corner", and it is drawn as one flat shape in one colour family so that it stays clear on a phone, in a browser toolbar and in print. Because it is built from the name itself, it can only be Amanfold.

The same two ideas run through the brand. Green is the colour every app already uses for "safe", so the brand colour and the good state agree. Cards, notes and other containers in the interface carry one folded corner.

02

Logo

Use the full logo wherever there is room, and the symbol alone where the name is already present, such as an app icon or a browser toolbar.

Three drawings for three jobs

How the name is drawn. The name is set in Readex Pro at weight 600 and then redrawn: both letters a are replaced by a round one-storey a that matches the symbol, spacing is 1.2% tighter, and the pairs n f, f o and l d are adjusted by hand. The symbol is as tall as the letters f, l and d, and the gap between symbol and name is one stem width. Always use the logo files, never type the name in a font.

Please don't

  • Mirror it, including in Arabic and other right-to-left layouts.
  • Recolour the letter or the fold outside the palette.
  • Stretch, squash, rotate or outline it.
  • Add padlocks, shields or keys. The keyhole already says it.

06

Voice and tone

Amanfold sounds like a calm, knowledgeable friend who happens to understand cryptography. It never tries to scare anyone into buying.

  • Calm, not alarming

    State what happened, then the next step. Security news is stressful enough without us adding to it.

  • Plain, not clever

    Say "password", not "credential". Explain how encryption works in one sentence when asked, and skip it when not.

  • Honest about limits

    Say what we cannot do. We cannot see your vault, so we cannot reset your master password. We are in beta, and we say so.

  • Equal in every language

    Arabic and every other language are written by people who think in them, not translated word for word. The same warmth in each.

We say

"Your vault is encrypted on your device. We never see what is inside."

"This password appeared in a public leak. Change it on example.com, then update it here."

"Free on every device you own. Premium adds secure file storage and emergency access."

We don't say

"Military-grade, unhackable, bank-level security."

"WARNING! You are at risk! Hackers could steal everything!"

"Upgrade now or lose your data."

Files

Everything on this page is generated from code in the repository, so the logo, icons and colours can never drift apart.

brand/logo/Symbol (full colour, one colour, small) and logos (Latin, Arabic, stacked) in colour, reversed, black and white, as SVG and PNG.
brand/app-icon/Store icon, rounded preview, Android adaptive and themed layers, favicon (SVG and ICO), PNG sizes 16 to 1024.
brand/icons/24 interface icons and a sprite sheet.
brand/tokens/Colours, type, spacing, radius and motion as CSS variables and JSON, for light and dark themes.
brand/tools/The scripts that build all of the above. Colours live in palette.py and the symbol's geometry in mark.py; change them and re-run to update every file.

Before launch: run a trademark similarity search and register the name and symbol (classes 9 and 42), because a keyhole inside a letter is not a new idea; have a native Arabic type designer check the Arabic name; and show the logo to a few real users.
