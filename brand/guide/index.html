01

The idea

Amanfold joins two words. Aman (أمان) is Arabic for safety, trust and peace of mind. Fold is what you do to keep something private.

Before envelopes existed, people sealed letters by folding them in on themselves and closing the last flap with wax or a paper lock. If anyone opened the letter on the way, the folds showed it. Historians call this letterlocking. It was everyday, end-to-end privacy, centuries before computers.

The Amanfold mark is that letter: a square sheet with its four corners folded to the centre, held shut by a saffron seal. It says what the product does without a padlock or a shield. Your passwords, cards and notes are folded shut on your own device, and only you hold the seal.

The same idea runs through the brand. Light and dark faces show the two sides of each fold. Containers in the interface carry one folded corner. Saffron appears once per screen, like a seal.

02

Logo

Use the full lockup wherever there is room, and the mark alone where the name is already present, such as an app icon or a browser toolbar.

Please don't

  • Rotate it into a diamond. It stops reading as a folded letter.
  • Recolour the flaps or the seal outside the palette.
  • Stretch, squash or skew it.
  • Add padlocks, shields or keys. The fold already says it.

Saved and sealed

One seal per screen

Apart from the seal in the logo itself, saffron appears at most once per screen, on its single most reassuring moment: a "sealed" confirmation, the folded corner of a payment card, the main button on a marketing page. If two things on a screen are saffron, one of them is wrong.

06

Voice and tone

Amanfold sounds like a calm, knowledgeable friend who happens to understand cryptography. It never tries to scare anyone into buying.

  • Calm, not alarming

    State what happened, then the next step. Security news is stressful enough without us adding to it.

  • Plain, not clever

    Say "password", not "credential". Explain how encryption works in one sentence when asked, and skip it when not.

  • Honest about limits

    Say what we cannot do. We cannot see your vault, so we cannot reset your master password. We are in beta, and we say so.

  • Equal in every language

    Arabic and every other language are written by people who think in them, not translated word for word. The same warmth in each.

We say

"Your vault is encrypted on your device. We never see what is inside."

"This password appeared in a public leak. Change it on example.com, then update it here."

"Free on every device you own. Premium adds secure file storage and emergency access."

We don't say

"Military-grade, unhackable, bank-level security."

"WARNING! You are at risk! Hackers could steal everything!"

"Upgrade now or lose your data."
