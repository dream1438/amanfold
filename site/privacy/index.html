Privacy notice

Last updated 28 November 2026

This notice covers this website and the beta waitlist. The Amanfold apps are zero-knowledge: the security page explains what our sync server can and cannot see. How security works

This website

This site uses no cookies, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party scripts or fonts. Everything loads from amanfold.com. Like any web server, ours sees your IP address when you visit, which is needed to send you the page. We do not use it to identify or follow you.

The beta waitlist

If you join the waitlist we store:

  • Your email address
  • The language you signed up in (English or Arabic)
  • The plan you said interests you
  • Whether you confirmed, and the date you signed up

We do not store your IP address or browser details with it. The confirmation link carries a random code that we keep only in scrambled (hashed) form.

Why, and for how long

We use your address only to invite you to the beta and send news about it. We never sell it or share it with advertisers. If you do not confirm within 7 days, it is deleted automatically. A confirmed address is kept until you leave the list or the beta ends.

Leaving the list

Every waitlist email has a link to leave. Opening it deletes your entry straight away. You can also write to us and we will delete it.

Who handles it

The waitlist is stored on our own server. Emails are sent through an email delivery provider, which receives your address only to deliver the message.

Your rights and contact

You can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or complain to your local data protection authority. Write to privacy@amanfold.com.
