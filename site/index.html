Why Amanfold

The problems people have with password managers, and what we do instead

We looked at what goes wrong for people who use password managers today. Each of these shaped how Amanfold is built.

  • The problemA company's servers get breached and encrypted vaults are stolen.

    What Amanfold doesYour vault is locked with your master password and a random Secret Key that only your devices hold. A stolen copy of our database cannot be guessed offline, even if your password is weak.

  • The problemFree plans stop working once you add a second device.

    What Amanfold doesFree means every phone, computer and browser you own, with sync. No device limit, ever.

  • The problemAutofill can be tricked into giving a password to a fake site.

    What Amanfold doesAmanfold fills only on the exact site you saved, never from a secure page onto an insecure one, and warns you when a site looks like a copy of one you use.

  • The problemForget the master password and everything is gone.

    What Amanfold doesYou get a printed recovery kit when you sign up. Families and teams can also name trusted people to help you back in.

  • The problemYou cannot see what the app really does with your data.

    What Amanfold doesThe apps, the encryption and the server are open source. Anyone can read the code and check our claims.

  • The problemArabic and right-to-left languages feel like an afterthought.

    What Amanfold doesAmanfold is written in English and Arabic from day one, with proper right-to-left layouts, and more languages are planned for launch.

  • The problemCards are stored like passwords, with numbers shown in full.

    What Amanfold doesCards are their own item type. Numbers stay hidden until you choose to see them, and the security code is never filled without asking.

  • The problemSharing with family or a team is fiddly or costs extra.

    What Amanfold doesShared vaults with clear roles. Before a vault opens, you compare a short safety number with the person, so nobody can slip their own key in.

What it keeps

Zero-knowledge

We built Amanfold so we cannot read your vault

Zero-knowledge means everything is locked on your device before it is sent, and the key never leaves you. If our servers were breached tomorrow, the attacker would get locked boxes.

  • Two secrets, not one

    Your vault is unlocked by your master password combined with a 128-bit Secret Key created on your device. Guessing the password alone gets an attacker nowhere.

  • Your password is never sent

    You sign in with OPAQUE, a modern protocol that proves you know your password without the password, or anything that could be used to guess it, reaching us.

  • Tamper-evident vaults

    Every change is signed by your own key. Your devices check each signature, so even a dishonest server could not swap, roll back or slip in items.

  • Open to inspection

    The design and the code are public. Our strength comes from your secrets and from well-studied encryption, not from hiding how it works.

Questions

Questions people ask

What happens if I forget my master password?

Use your recovery kit: the Secret Key and 24 recovery words you print or save when you sign up, plus a code we email you. We cannot reset your password for you, because we never have it. That is the point.

If the code is public, does that make it easier to hack?

No. Banks and messaging apps use the same published encryption methods. What keeps your vault safe is your master password and Secret Key, which are never in the code and never sent to us. Publishing the code lets independent experts find mistakes, so they get fixed instead of hidden.

Is it safe to use during the beta?

The encryption is built from standard, well-reviewed parts, and our internal review is public. But no outside firm has audited it yet. Use it, tell us what breaks, and keep another copy of anything you cannot afford to lose until the beta label comes off.

Can I move my passwords in and out?

Import from other password managers and browsers, and export of everything you have saved, are on the beta roadmap. Your data will always be yours to take with you, in an open format.

Which devices does it work on?

Right now: the browser extension for Chrome, Edge and Firefox. iPhone, iPad and Android apps are in development and will join the beta as they are ready.

Who is behind Amanfold?

Amanfold is a small, independent project. Aman (أمان) is Arabic for safety. We are building it in the open, and the source code is on GitHub for anyone to read.
